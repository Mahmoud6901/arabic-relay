Set up dictation
Arabic Relay turns your voice into text on this computer. It needs a speech model once; after that, dictation works offline.
No NVIDIA GPU, so transcription will run on the CPU and take a few seconds per sentence.


Your personal vocabulary
When self-learning is enabled, after you review your transcript and click Prepare English prompt, the app remembers the English words you typed or corrected and short spelling corrections. Words speech recognition heard and you left alone aren't saved. Saved words guide English spelling checks, and corrections can become rules you approve. They aren't given to the speech model, because that made it write mixed speech as English.
Only words and correction pairs are saved on this computer, not complete transcripts. Saved hints also go to the prompt model when it checks English spellings. Up to 300 words and 100 corrections are kept.
